## Slide outline (16 slides)

1. **Cover** — Senddot, agency-first outbound infrastructure. IP acquisition opportunity, $75,000.
2. **The opportunity in one page** — what is being sold, why now, headline asking price.
3. **Problem** — agencies running outbound at scale burn domains and mailboxes; per-seat SaaS caps them and owns their data.
4. **What Senddot is** — self-hosted, white-label, multi-client outbound infrastructure the owner controls end to end.
5. **Platform architecture** — app layer, database with strict per-agency isolation, background sending worker, edge functions, SMTP transport (diagram).
6. **Core feature set 1: sending engine** — queue-first sending, batch processing every minute, SMTP rotation pool, per-account daily/hourly quotas, staggered scheduling to avoid volume spikes, SMTP-to-sender-identity pairing.
7. **Core feature set 2: deliverability** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC verification, domain reputation checks, warm-up staging, per-account deliverability confidence meter and sender risk indicator, bounce/failure diagnostics.
8. **Core feature set 3: pipeline** — lead finder with web scraping, business directory persistence, contact import with column mapping, built-in CRM with conversion funnel.
9. **Core feature set 4: AI and automation** — AI email writing assistant (tones and purposes), AI personalized outreach from a prospect's own website, webhooks and tokens for Zapier/n8n/HubSpot.
10. **Real-life outbound applicability** — mapped use cases: agency running outbound for 5+ clients, SDR teams, lead-gen shops, recruiters, B2B founders; each with how the rotation pool and per-client isolation is used day to day.
11. **Production proof (real platform data)** — 118 campaigns, all 118 completed, 3,220 emails delivered through the queue, 1,736 tracked opens, 637 tracked clicks, 2,930 contacts, 2,391 directory records, 2,089 CRM leads, 12 SMTP accounts and 19 sender identities across 5 client workspaces, 9 accounts, live from Mar 2026 to Aug 2026. Failures are shown honestly and attributed (e.g. a mailbox disabled at the email host, not a platform fault) to build credibility rather than hide it.
12. **Pilot programme and commercial history** — pilot account programme, current commercial model (one-time deployment $30,000 / ₹25,00,000 plus monthly maintenance, and a ₹3,499 / $49 monthly subscription tier) as evidence of a proven, priced product.
13. **Monetisation paths for the buyer** — resell deployments, run it as SaaS, license per agency, white-label to sub-brands. Simple illustrative maths using the existing published prices only, clearly labelled as illustrative.
14. **What's included in the $75,000** — complete source code, database schema and migrations, background workers and edge functions, exclusivity, resale rights, rebrand rights, domains, professional email addresses, historical campaign results and pilot data, handover and onboarding support.
15. **Handover plan** — code transfer, environment deployment, domain/email transfer, walkthrough sessions, support window.
16. **Close** — the ask: $75,000 for full IP ownership. Contact details (support@senddot.in, ssingh2100.2100@gmail.com, +91-7609933502).
